What we built

Six chapters

0D → 1D → 2D → 3D → 4D → M: amino-acid focus, sequence, local secondary motifs, tertiary context, conformational change over time, and multiscale connections.

Musical progression

One through five interacting musical voices, original notes and three timbres. An illustrative 4D distance crossing changes consonance and dissonance.

Accessible exploration

Four user-chosen sensory profiles, written descriptions, optional narration, haptic requests, and touch/pen-compatible controls with a local evidence record.
